# ISO 27001 Requirements Summarised: Clauses 4 to 10

The mandatory requirements of ISO 27001 fit in seven clauses. Knowing them makes the rest of the standard easier to follow.

## A clause-by-clause summary

- Clause 4 covers context and scope; clause 5 covers leadership and policy.
- Clause 6 covers planning, including risk assessment and the Statement of Applicability.
- Clauses 7 and 8 cover support and operation of the controls.
- Clauses 9 and 10 cover performance evaluation, internal audit, management review and improvement.

Annex A controls are chosen from your risk assessment, so they are not all mandatory.

## Why this matters

People often assume all of Annex A is mandatory. The mandatory part of the standard is clauses 4 to 10. Annex A is a reference you apply according to your risks.

## A practical next step

Keep the scope, policy, risk results, Statement of Applicability, audit and review records together in one place. Auditors will ask for them early.
